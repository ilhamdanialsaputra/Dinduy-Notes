Dinduy is a fast, private notes app. Your notes are stored on your phone.

• Rich text: bold, italic, lists, checklists, quotes, code, tables and links
• Images with cropping, covers and per-note themes
• Read PDFs, export notes to PDF
• Handwriting and highlighter drawn right on the page
• Reminders (one-time or repeating) and home-screen widgets
• Voice notes with on-device transcription (Whisper). Optional cloud transcription with your own Groq API key
• Scan text from photos (OCR) fully on-device; language data is downloaded once, only after you agree
• Labels, pinning, archive, trash, search, Markdown export, share notes as .dinduy files
• Material You colors, light and AMOLED dark themes

No ads, no trackers, no accounts. The app never installs or updates itself.
